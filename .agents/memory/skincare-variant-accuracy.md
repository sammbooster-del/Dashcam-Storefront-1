---
name: Skincare variant accuracy
description: Why catalog descriptions must verify the exact pimple-patch formula and pack variant.
---

Verify skincare ingredients, pack counts and included accessories against the manufacturer's exact product variant, rather than copying comparison articles wholesale.

**Why:** Current pimple-patch roundups incorrectly attributed active ingredients to plain hydrocolloid products. Manufacturer comparison tables can also include claims for neighboring products. Classic yellow stars, medicated stars, refill packs and compact kits are not interchangeable.

**How to apply:** Distinguish plain hydrocolloid patches from salicylic-acid and microdart variants, and state when a refill excludes its compact. Describe modest, supported benefits for superficial whiteheads and protection against picking; do not promise an acne cure or treatment of deep cysts.
