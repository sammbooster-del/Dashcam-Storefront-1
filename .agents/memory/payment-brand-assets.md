---
name: Payment brand asset contrast
description: Google Pay logo variants are named for their intended backgrounds, not their lettering color.
---

Google's `light_gpay.svg` has dark Pay lettering for a light background. `dark_gpay.svg` has white Pay lettering for a dark background.

**Why:** Using the dark variant on a white button left only the colored G visible and made the logo look incomplete, even though the asset loaded successfully.

**How to apply:** Choose the variant by button background and verify both the G and Pay lettering remain visible. Do not treat a successful asset load as sufficient visual verification.
