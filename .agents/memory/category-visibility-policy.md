---
name: Category visibility policy
description: Why category switches must remain separate from individual product visibility.
---

A disabled category is a visibility override, not a bulk update to its products.

**Why:** Re-enabling a category must not expose products that were individually hidden. New products added to a disabled category must also remain hidden until the owner enables that category.

**How to apply:** Preserve individual visibility and stock. Exclude disabled-category products from browsing, search, direct product pages and new checkout orders. Keep the admin catalog and historical orders intact, and make admin visibility labels and counts reflect the category override.
