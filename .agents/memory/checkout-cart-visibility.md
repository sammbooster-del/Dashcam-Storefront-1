---
name: Checkout cart visibility
description: Product visibility requirement for mobile checkout layout decisions
---

Show cart products directly on mobile checkout; do not hide them behind a collapsed order-summary control.

**Why:** The user objected when products required opening a dropdown to be seen in checkout. Shortening the page is less important than confirming what is being purchased at a glance.

**How to apply:** When changing the checkout layout, keep product image/name, quantity, and total visible without interaction, while making the form easy to reach below.

Offer a direct Buy now path from the product page that adds the selected item and opens the delivery form. Keep Add to cart as a separate option for shoppers who want to continue browsing.

**Why:** The user found adding an item, scrolling to the cart icon, opening the cart, and scrolling back to the form stressful. They explicitly chose a separate Buy now action rather than changing what Add to cart does.

**How to apply:** Preserve the direct path without hiding the cart contents or removing the browsing path. Do not replace an existing cart without asking.

Keep clear Back links on secondary storefront pages and easy Add to cart actions on product listings and mobile product pages.

**Why:** The owner asked for Back and easy Add to cart buttons everywhere necessary.

**How to apply:** Use safe in-store destinations for Back links so direct page visits also work. Require an option choice before adding multi-option products; keep Buy now separate and unchanged.

Keep delivery first. At payment, let the shopper select a payment method and click Next before revealing card and billing fields. Back navigation must preserve entered delivery details.

**Why:** The owner requested selection before payment details, rejected a new payment-selection screen before delivery, and asked for a modern checkout rather than another upfront step.

**How to apply:** Keep card fields out of the method-selection view; show the disabled coming-soon wallets there alongside the usable card choice. Do not move this selector ahead of delivery without clarification.

Show a loading spinner for a few seconds before advancing checkout steps, including after submitting delivery details with Next.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a visible pause instead of immediate step changes.

**How to apply:** Validate first, then show the transition while blocking duplicate clicks. Keep entered details intact and respect reduced-motion preferences without eliminating the requested pause.
